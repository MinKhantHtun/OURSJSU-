CMPE 50 - Intro to Data Structures and Object-Oriented Programming

4 unit(s)
Application of object-oriented software engineering techniques to the design and development of larger programs; data abstraction, structures, classes, arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, and associated algorithms.

Lecture 3 hours/lab 3 hours.

Prerequisite(s): CMPE 30  (with a minimum grade of “C-“).  
Grading: Letter Graded
